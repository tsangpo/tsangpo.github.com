Readict Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

Readict is a local dictionary and EPUB reading app.

Data collection

Readict does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Local files and app data

Readict lets you select local MDX, MDD, and EPUB files through the Android system file picker. The app stores file access permissions, dictionary configuration, reading position, lookup history, and favorite words locally on your device so the app can provide its dictionary and reading features.

Network access

Readict does not use a remote dictionary account, analytics service, advertising service, or tracking service.

Data sharing

Readict does not share user data with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove dictionaries or books in the app, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Security

Readict stores app data locally using Android app storage. User-selected files remain under Android's file access controls.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact listed on Readict's Google Play store listing.
